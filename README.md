# North Sumatra Travel

> Premium guided tour platform for Lake Toba and the cultural heartland of North Sumatra, Indonesia.

**[Live Demo →](https://north-sumatra-travel.vercel.app)**

---

## About

North Sumatra Travel is a travel booking website for curated tour packages around Lake Toba — the world's largest volcanic lake, formed by a supervolcanic eruption 74,000 years ago. The site targets international and regional tourists seeking authentic, expert-guided experiences rooted in Batak culture and natural landscapes.

---

## Features

### Curated Itineraries
Two professionally designed 4-day tour packages:

**Classic Route — Medan → Lake Toba → Berastagi**
- Day-by-day breakdown of cultural and scenic stops
- Focused on immersive Batak heritage experiences
- Includes Samosir Island cultural village visits

**Explorer Route — Lake Toba & Waterfall Focus**
- Emphasis on Samosir Island and natural waterfall experiences
- Off-the-beaten-path stops for adventurous travelers
- Includes local guide storytelling at each site

### Interactive Booking System
- Select preferred itinerary (Classic or Explorer)
- Pick travel dates with a date picker
- Choose group size from 1 to 6+ people
- Streamlined booking request form

### Journey Map
Visual route representation showing all four destination stops across the itinerary — lets travelers see the full journey at a glance before booking.

### Social Proof & Credentials
- 500+ happy travelers served
- 4.9-star average rating
- 6+ years of operating experience
- Guides certified in Batak heritage and cultural history
- Testimonials from international visitors (Malaysia, Singapore, India, Australia)

### Destination Education
- Rich geological context about Lake Toba's formation
- Cultural background on the Batak people and their traditions
- Integrated into section content, not just a facts page
